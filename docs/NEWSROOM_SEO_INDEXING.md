## Google Search Console (owner action required)

1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Add a URL-prefix property for `https://theraees4hmad-debug.github.io/CreatorBoost/`.
3. Complete Google's ownership verification using one of the methods offered for the GitHub Pages property.
4. Open Sitemaps and submit `sitemap.xml`.
5. Use URL Inspection on the newsroom homepage and a sample article URL, then request indexing.
6. Revisit Page indexing and Sitemaps reports after Google crawls the site.

CreatorBoost cannot verify ownership of the Google account or submit indexing requests without the site owner's Search Console access. A sitemap submission is a discovery hint, not a guarantee of indexing or ranking.

## Current sitemap contents

The committed sitemap includes the current published newsroom article URLs. Future articles remain discoverable through the newsroom listing; refresh the sitemap when the published article inventory changes.

## Social sharing

Article pages expose share links for WhatsApp, Facebook, X, Telegram, LinkedIn, Reddit, Threads, Pinterest, email and the device share sheet. Instagram, TikTok and YouTube are offered through native sharing/copy-link because their direct share/publish flows require platform-specific app capabilities or authorized APIs.
